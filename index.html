A bit about me

My workdays are full of campaigns, decks, spreadsheets and a lot of calls.

At some point I started wondering how much of that I could hand off to a tool I made myself. That turned into a habit. Now whenever something annoys me at work, or I get an idea I can't stop thinking about, I try to build it.

Some of these I use every week. Some exist because football and puzzles are fun. All of them taught me something.
